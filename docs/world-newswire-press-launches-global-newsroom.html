World Newswire Press Launches: A Global Newsroom Built to Give the World a Voice

A new international news site and wire service goes live today, covering five regions of the planet, publishing its own reporting, and opening a Press Room where organizations anywhere can distribute their news.

By the World Desk, World Newswire PressNovember 21, 2026, 15:45 UTCWorld

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 21, 2026 — World Newswire Press, a global news site and wire service, went live today at worldnewswirepress.com. It covers the world through five regional desks (the Americas, Europe, Africa, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific) and three beats that cross every border: business, entertainment, and faith and culture.

Its founding idea fits in five words: giving the world a voice. Much of the planet is reported on from a distance, if it is reported on at all. World Newswire Press is built to carry reporting from the places the story is actually happening, and to make it simple for the people there to reach an editor.

What readers will find

The homepage pairs the World Desk's own reporting with a live wire of headlines from more than thirty of the world's newsrooms, from the BBC, Al Jazeera and DW to Africanews, the South China Morning Post and the Japan Times. Every wire headline is credited to the outlet that published it and links to its story there. World Newswire Press does not republish other outlets' articles.

The site refreshes every hour. A bar of world clocks runs from Los Angeles to Sydney, and an "Explore the world" band lets readers go straight to any region.

A wire service, too

Through its Press Room, organizations anywhere can distribute news releases worldwide, starting at $99. Releases are labeled as releases, sponsored material is always labeled as sponsored, and nothing is published without a named editor's sign-off.

An open door

Readers can send a confidential tip; the tip page opens the reader's own email app, so nothing is stored on the website. Reporters, photographers and stringers on every continent can apply to become correspondents, with terms agreed in writing before any assignment.

World Newswire Press is part of the Newswire Hollywood media group. Its standards and corrections policy is published on the About page.
