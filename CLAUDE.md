# WORLD NEWSWIRE PRESS — STANDING RULES

These load at the start of every session in this repo.

## WHAT THIS IS

World Newswire Press (worldnewswirepress.com) is the flagship global news site
and wire service of the Newswire Hollywood media group. Terry Bryant is the
editor. Terry directs, Claude executes. Mission: **giving the world a voice.**

The Newswire Hollywood standing rules apply here too: plain English, teach one
idea per task (log it in runbook/learning-log.md), gap analysis before new
builds, copy-paste-ready output, zero-cost infrastructure, commit everything,
never leave work on a branch (Pages serves from main).

## HOW THE SITE IS BUILT — read before editing anything in docs/

GENERATED by wire.py every hour (never hand-edit, the next run overwrites):
index.html, americas.html, europe.html, africa.html, middle-east.html,
asia-pacific.html, business.html, entertainment.html, faith-culture.html,
wire.html, feed.xml, and every story page in docs/s/ (see the trial below).
sitemaps.py writes sitemap.xml and news-sitemap.xml.

HAND-WRITTEN (wire.py only refreshes the shared header and footer between the
WNP:HEADER / WNP:FOOTER markers, and the Press Room release list between the
WNP:RELEASES markers): pressroom.html, tips.html, partners.html, about.html
and every article page.

To change what appears:
- Our own stories and releases: add an entry to data/originated.json and the
  article page to docs/ (or drafts/ if embargoed). Copy an existing article
  page as the starting point: it already has the fact box, closing question,
  sources list, photo credit, NewsArticle JSON-LD and the markers.
- Feeds we pull: data/feeds.json (section "world" = routed by place names).
- Image licenses: data/image-credits.json. Every image records its source.
- Layout: the template functions in wire.py; styling in docs/assets/site.css.

## THE HARD RULES THE CODE ENFORCES

1. No signed_off_by => the story is held: on no page, feed or sitemap.
2. Embargo in the future => held the same way, page kept in drafts/.
3. Wire cards show another outlet's headline, their own feed photo credited to
   them, and a link out. Never their article text. Story pages add only the
   outlet's own feed teaser (trimmed to ~320 characters, credited to them);
   wire.py never fetches or copies an article body.
4. Never invent wire items. Empty wire => the honest "refreshing" note.
5. Sponsored content carries a visible Sponsored label; releases are labeled.
6. Licensed images only: original, public domain, CC with credit, or written
   permission.
7. Social links: Instagram and Facebook constants in wire.py. YOUTUBE is empty
   until the channel exists. No links to other sites' social accounts.

## STORY PAGES TRIAL

Story pages trial: started Nov 21 2026, review Dec 6 2026; restore point
branch restore-before-story-pages-2026-11-21; to roll back set
STORY_PAGES = False (top of wire.py) and push. The next hourly run makes every
wire card link straight to the outlet again and deletes docs/s/.

While it is on, every wire headline shown on the site gets a page at
docs/s/<slug>.html (slug = short title + 8-character fingerprint of the
item's link, so the address is stable). Cards open that page in the same
tab and keep a small "Read at <outlet>" link that goes straight out. Each
story page: their headline, their feed photo credited "Photo: <outlet>" (or
a navy Blue Marble band), their own feed teaser, a big "Read the full story"
button, our best-matching World Desk story, related headlines, ad slot and
follow box. Every story page is noindex, its canonical points at the
original article, and it is never in sitemap.xml, news-sitemap.xml or
feed.xml. Pages for headlines that leave the wire are deleted on the next
run; the live count is in runbook/wire-feed-status.md. Never hand-edit
docs/s/: it is regenerated every hour.

## STORIES

Byline "By the World Desk, World Newswire Press". Every story: confirmed facts
only, own words (never copy sentences), fact box, closing question, sources
list with links, credited licensed photo, named sign-off.
