Work with us Correspondents & partners

Build the world’s newsroom with us

World Newswire Press is built to carry reporting from where the news is happening. We work with independent journalists, photographers and news organizations on every continent.

Correspondents worldwide

Reporters, photographers, videographers, fixers and stringers: if you are on the ground somewhere the world should be watching, we want to hear from you. Send a short introduction, where you are based, the languages you work in, links to published work, and one or two story ideas.

Terms first, always. Every assignment, fee, deadline and rights arrangement is agreed in writing before any work begins. We do not ask anyone to work on spec or to take risks for a story.

News organizations

Newsrooms, wire services and regional outlets can exchange headlines, share coverage of major events, and co-report stories that cross borders. Every exchange is credited and linked both ways.
