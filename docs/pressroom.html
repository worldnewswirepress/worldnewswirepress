Press Room Wire distribution

Put your news in front of the world

World Newswire Press distributes news releases from organizations on every continent: companies, governments, charities, artists, faith groups and campaigns. Every release is clearly labeled, carried on our site and feed, and approved by a named editor before it runs.

Distribution tiers

Standard$99per release
  • Published in the World Newswire Press Press Room, labeled as a press release
  • Carried in our RSS feed and submitted to search engines
  • One photo (with your written permission) and up to two links
  • Permanent page with your media contact
  • Live link sent to you the moment it publishes
Submit a Standard release
RecommendedFeatured$299per release
  • Everything in Standard
  • Placed in the matching region or topic section of the site
  • Shared on our Instagram and Facebook, labeled as a release
  • Up to four photos and a video link
  • An editor’s check of headline and summary for clarity
Submit a Featured release
Syndicated$599per release
  • Everything in Featured
  • Offered to our syndication and newsroom partners for pickup
  • Cross-posted across the Newswire Hollywood media group where the subject fits
  • A distribution report with every live link
  • Scheduling to the minute for embargoed news
Submit a Syndicated release

Prices in US dollars. A release buys distribution, not coverage: our newsroom decides independently whether to report on any subject, and paid releases never influence that decision.

How to submit

  1. Email the releaseSend it to info@worldnewswirepress.com with the tier you want in the subject line. Paste the text into the email or attach a Word or PDF file.
  2. Add photos you have the right to useOriginal photos, or press images with written permission from the owner. Tell us who to credit. We do not run images without a clear license.
  3. Tell us the timingPublish now, or a precise embargo time with the time zone. Embargoes are hard stops: nothing goes out early.
  4. Name a media contactA person, an email and a phone number that journalists can actually reach.
  5. We confirm and invoiceAn editor replies to confirm the tier, the timing and the price before anything is published.
  6. Editor sign-off, then liveA named editor approves the release. You get the live link, and for Syndicated, the full distribution report.

The rules every release follows

  • Every release is labeled as a press release, and paid or sponsored material is labeled as sponsored. Always.
  • Nothing publishes without a named editor’s sign-off. We may edit for clarity and format, and we may decline any release.
  • No false claims, no hate, no content that puts people at risk. We do not publish releases attacking private individuals.
  • Your release stays your words. Our newsroom’s reporting stays ours.
